The problem

Evolve runs six divisions: agri-business trade, marketing services, boreholes and solar, transport and logistics, business advisory, and plots and houses. Leads, follow-ups, stock and sales lived in Excel files, notebooks and WhatsApp chats.

Follow-ups were missed, nobody could see the full pipeline, and repeat customers were not reminded when it was time to reorder. The company had no accounting or business software at all.

What the system does

CRM: customers and sales

Every lead tagged to its division, with status and value. A log of every meeting, call, site visit and message. Next steps with dates that create follow-up reminders automatically, and reorder alerts based on each customer's buying cycle.

ERP: operations and finance

Stock and purchasing, sales and invoicing for a VAT-registered business, jobs and projects, and expenses and finance, in the same system as the customers they belong to.

Secure logins and roles

Staff sign in with their own accounts. The CEO and I are administrators, so the system keeps running if either of us is away, while staff see only what they need.

Private and online

Hosted online so the team can use it from any district, with the code kept in a private repository and the database owned and managed by me.

How I built it

01 Map the business

Worked out how each division finds customers, sells and follows up, then designed the data around that.

02 Ship the CRM first

Put a working first version live in two weeks so the sales team could start using it straight away.

03 Grow it into an ERP

Added operations and finance modules on the same database, so customers, stock and money connect.

04 Hand over safely

Set up a backup administrator and clear ownership so the company is never locked out of its own data.
